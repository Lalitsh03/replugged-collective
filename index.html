Why RPC

Screens are easy. Presence takes practice.

Replugged Collective exists because "put your phone down" is easy advice and hard to actually do. We make it easy by putting something better in your hands, literally. Every box is a reason to sit at a table, use your fingers, and finish something that isn't a scroll.

No hobby expertise required. No pressure to post about it. Just a monthly nudge to unplug from your feed and plug into something real: a room, a craft, a small win you can hold.

What's in the box

  • All materials for one hobby, sized for a first try
  • A simple guide, no prior skill needed
  • A prompt to log off and start, no timer, no rush
  • A note on where the hobby can go next, if you're hooked
